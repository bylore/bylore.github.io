Project

Generalized Nonlocal Operators

This project studies generalized nonlocal operators as a mathematical basis for continuum models that extend beyond local differential formulations. The focus is on operator definition, constitutive interpretation, and rigorous relationships between nonlocal and local limits.

The work connects abstract operator structure with modeling questions in solid mechanics, especially where interfaces, defects, and discontinuities make classical local descriptions incomplete or inconvenient.
