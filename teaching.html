Current Teaching

Graduate course Tongji University

Advanced Computational Mechanics

This course introduces finite difference, finite element, and finite volume methods, with emphasis on error analysis, convergence, and stability, while connecting local discretization ideas to nonlocal integral equations, meshfree methods, and PINNs.

Recent Teaching

Spring 2023
Undergraduate course Tongji University

Classical Mechanics

An introduction to mechanics foundations with attention to kinematics, balance laws, and the bridge from analytical models to computational interpretation.

Teaching Approach

Teaching is organized around mathematical structure and its computational consequences: students are guided from mechanics foundations and variational thinking to numerical formulation, so that theory, modeling, and implementation remain visibly connected rather than taught as separate layers.
